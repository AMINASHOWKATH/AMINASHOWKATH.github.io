IS Integration Maturity in Latvian PMOs

A self-developed four-level Integration Maturity Model (IMM), tested against 40 respondents across 7 Latvian industry sectors, with one expert interview and full statistical validation in SPSS.

Methodology & findings

Approach

  • Designed a 4-level Integration Maturity Model (IMM) from scratch
  • 40-respondent survey across 7 industry sectors
  • One anonymised expert interview for qualitative grounding
  • Spearman rank correlation analysis in SPSS

Key results

  • H1 confirmed: 70% of PMOs sampled sit at IMM Levels 2–3
  • Integration maturity correlated with reporting automation (r=0.405)
  • Correlated with data quality (r=0.321) and governance standardisation (r=0.403)
